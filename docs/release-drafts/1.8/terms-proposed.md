Local draft for owner and legal review. This file is outside the website's published content. It does not change the effective terms, an App Store EULA selection, or any price. Set the effective/update date only when publication is separately authorized. The live pricing hold in `docs/paid-tracking-rollout.md` still applies.

The sections below propose replacements for the corresponding sections at `https://whereday.com/terms`. Keep the remaining sections subject to legal review. Do not treat this document as an Apple-approved custom EULA or legal sign-off.

## Proposed section 02: Description of service

Whereday is a location journal for iPhone and Apple Watch. It records visits and routes so you can review your history on your devices and export saved records. Whereday does not require a Whereday account for location tracking and does not operate a server archive of your location history.

Some features use network services. Apple handles purchases, subscription status, purchase restoration, and Family Sharing. Maps, geocoding, optional export destinations, and export scheduling have the network behavior described in our [Privacy Policy](https://whereday.com/privacy). Location permissions and supported devices are required for recording; a purchase does not bypass system permissions.

## Proposed section 04: Access, purchases, and subscriptions

### Existing access

Users who first obtained Whereday before the subscription update keep their existing free tracking access. Existing free users still need the previously required export purchase to unlock export features. Earlier lifetime purchases remain valid, including Family Sharing on the original lifetime unlock. No subscription is required to preserve this existing tracking access or an earlier lifetime purchase.

Apple purchase history and existing installation records determine eligibility for this retained access. Restoring purchases or confirming eligibility on another device may require the Apple Account associated with the original access and a network connection. Contact support if previously held access is not recognized. Restoring access does not restore a deleted location journal.

### New-user access

Users who first obtain Whereday with the subscription update or later must start an eligible subscription trial or hold a valid purchase before starting to track. New users can choose Individual or Family access through a weekly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a separate one-time Lifetime purchase. There is no monthly subscription.

### Trials, payment, and renewal

Eligible weekly and yearly subscriptions offer a 7-day free trial. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple and is not guaranteed for every Apple Account or every plan change. A trial begins only after you confirm the subscription with Apple; downloading Whereday or completing onboarding does not start a trial. Lifetime purchases do not include a subscription trial.

Unless canceled, a subscription renews automatically at the price and billing interval shown by Apple before confirmation. After a free trial, Apple charges the price for the selected weekly or yearly plan. Payments are charged to your Apple Account under Apple's purchase terms. Local currency, applicable taxes, prices, and renewal information appear in Apple's purchase confirmation. The account may be charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings. To avoid the next renewal charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or subscription period ends. Follow any earlier cancellation deadline shown by Apple for your offer or storefront. Deleting the app, stopping tracking, disabling location permissions, or removing your data does not cancel a subscription. Access follows the valid entitlement and expiration or revocation status supplied by Apple. Refunds and any rights required by applicable consumer law are handled under Apple's policies and applicable law.

Each subscription group is subject to Apple's introductory-offer eligibility rules. Switching plans does not promise another free trial. Apple displays the billing effect of a plan change before confirmation. Buying a Lifetime product does not itself cancel an existing auto-renewing subscription; manage that subscription with Apple to avoid unwanted renewal.

### Lifetime purchases and Family Sharing

Lifetime access is a separate one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. It does not renew automatically. Existing lifetime purchases continue to cover their purchased features. A Family Lifetime Upgrade adds Family Sharing to an eligible Individual Lifetime purchase; it is not an additional recurring subscription.

Family products allow eligible access to be shared with up to five other family members through Apple Family Sharing. Eligibility, supported storefronts, and subscription or purchase sharing settings are controlled by Apple. Individual products are not family-shareable. Sharing a purchase does not share a person's location journal with other family members. Each person's journal remains on their devices unless they choose to export or share it.

### Records after access expires

For users who joined under the subscription model, existing records remain available to view and export after a trial or subscription expires. Starting new tracking or adding a manual visit requires active access. Expiration alone does not delete existing records. Existing users with retained free tracking keep that access and their earlier export purchase requirements.

Purchase restoration restores eligible access, not local data. Records can still be lost through app deletion, device loss, or other storage failures. Keep backups of records you need to retain.

### Prices and refunds

The price, currency, tax treatment, product, and renewal terms shown by the App Store before confirmation control the purchase. These terms do not announce a future price increase or guarantee a price across storefronts. Price changes are subject to Apple's processes and applicable law. Apple processes purchases and refund requests; contact Apple for billing or refund assistance and Whereday support for access-recognition problems. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights.

## Proposed section 07: Data and backups

Your location journal is stored locally on your devices. Whereday does not provide a server copy that can recover it if the app or its data is deleted, or if a device is lost or damaged. Keep exported backups of records you need. Any recovery through device backups depends on the backup system you use; Whereday does not guarantee that such a backup exists or can be restored.

Restoring an Apple purchase or subscription restores eligible feature access only. It does not recover deleted visits, routes, photos, or exports. Ending a subscription does not by itself remove local records, but it does not protect them from device or storage loss.

If you configure webhook delivery or automatic exports, copies go to the destinations you choose. You are responsible for the security and handling of those copies. See our [Privacy Policy](https://whereday.com/privacy) for the services and limited technical metadata used by the app.

## Proposed section 11: Stopping use

You may stop using Whereday at any time. To stop an auto-renewing subscription, cancel it separately in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting Whereday does not cancel billing through Apple.

Any discontinuation or material change to the service remains subject to applicable law and existing purchase rights. These terms do not remove the retained free tracking access or prior lifetime entitlements described above.
