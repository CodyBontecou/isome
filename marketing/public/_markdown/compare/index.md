# Choose how you keep your location history.

Understand the account, recording, backup, and export choices before changing the way you keep your own visits and routes.

By [Cody Bontecou](https://isolated.tech), Whereday’s developer · Reviewed October 30, 2026

## [Whereday and Google Timeline](https://whereday.com/compare/google-maps-timeline/)

A comparison of recording controls, device storage, backup, export formats, and payment. Learn where each fits and why your existing Google archive needs to be kept separately.

[Read the Google Timeline comparison](https://whereday.com/compare/google-maps-timeline/)

## Moving an existing Google archive?

Choose the [Google Timeline import walkthrough](https://whereday.com/blog/import-google-timeline-iphone/) if you already have a supported JSON file and want its visits and timed points in Whereday. Version 1.8 previews new records, approximate duplicates, and skipped entries before saving. Keep your original archive: importing selected records does not reproduce every Google activity or replace Google’s device-transfer backup.

For records already in Whereday, use the [backup and restore guide](https://whereday.com/blog/back-up-iphone-location-history/). A GPX route for a viewer and a restorable journal copy are different outputs.

## New to recording your own history?

Start with [the private location journal guide](https://whereday.com/blog/private-location-journal-iphone/), or check [which files Whereday can export and restore](https://whereday.com/docs/export-import/).
