# Contact iso.me

Get help with iso.me or talk to the developer about the project.

By [Cody Bontecou](https://isolated.tech), iso.me’s developer · Reviewed October 26, 2026

## Support by email

Email [cody.bontecou@gmail.com](mailto:cody.bontecou@gmail.com) to reach Cody Bontecou, the developer of iso.me. Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, the app version, and your iOS or watchOS version. If a screenshot helps explain the issue, remove personal details before sharing it.

You can ask for help without sending your location records, precise coordinates, private export files, or a full personal journal. If a small example becomes useful during troubleshooting, use invented locations and share only the part needed to reproduce the behavior. This is a support channel, so a response time is not guaranteed.

## Public project conversations

Report reproducible bugs in the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/CodyBontecou/isome/issues). For feature ideas and general project questions, use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/CodyBontecou/isome/discussions). Posts there are public, so remove sensitive information before posting.

For setup and product questions, start with the [documentation](https://isome.isolated.tech/docs/). The [privacy policy](https://isome.isolated.tech/privacy) explains data handling, and the [terms](https://isome.isolated.tech/terms) describe purchases and use of the app. App Store purchases and refunds are processed through Apple; do not send payment credentials by email.
