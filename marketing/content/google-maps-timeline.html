An alternative for your next recorded day

iso.me is an iPhone location journal for people who want to record their own visits and routes without a Google account. Recording is free. A one-time purchase unlocks file exports, scheduled exports, and webhooks.

Google Timeline already keeps history on your device and offers encrypted backup. Choose based on the recording and export workflow you need. Starting iso.me does not move your old Timeline archive.

When iso.me fits

  • You want a personal route and visit journal without creating another account.
  • You want to choose when recording starts and stops, review outings, and replay recorded routes.
  • You need GPX for a route viewer, CSV for a spreadsheet, or Markdown for a readable archive.
  • You want an optional webhook workflow alongside a local journal.

Use the private location journal guide to set up your first recording. Start enables detailed GPS as well as visit monitoring; there is no separate passive visit-only switch. Permissions, GPS conditions, and battery can affect the record.

When keeping Google Timeline makes sense

Keep Timeline if its existing archive, Maps workflow, and backup meet your needs. iso.me is not a drop-in replacement for importing that archive. Review Google’s current settings and backup instructions before changing your setup.

If you want to try iso.me, record a short session and inspect it before relying on it for a longer trip. You can evaluate the free recording features before buying export access.

Preserve your existing history first

Save Google’s exported archive separately before changing apps or devices. App Store 1.7.16 cannot import that archive. A future importer still cannot reconstruct records missing from the file.

For existing Apple or Google records, start with the iPhone history guide. For new iso.me routes, follow the GPX export walkthrough and check export and restore compatibility. A GPX file is useful for drawing a route elsewhere, but it cannot be imported into iso.me. Keep supported visit and point exports for records you want to restore later.

Understand what local storage means

iso.me does not operate a server archive of your location history. Apple services handle place names and map features; first-party onboarding and purchase telemetry is enabled by default and excludes your location records. Optional webhooks deliver records to an endpoint you configure. Exported copies follow the storage rules of the destination you choose.

Read the privacy policy, recording controls, and current pricing before choosing a workflow.
